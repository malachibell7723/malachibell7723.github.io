ABOUT ME

Building the connection between hardware and software.

I am learning C and developing my skills in other programming languages with the goal of programming chips and microcontrollers. My focus is embedded systems: writing the software that helps electronic hardware read sensors, control devices, and respond to the world around it.

Completing CS50x and building my ESP32 RFID-controlled safe box are steps toward that goal. I am continuing to learn, practice, and build projects that connect my electrical experience with programming.
